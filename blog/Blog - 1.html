Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a solar system cost in Kottayam?

The cost depends on the system size, roof type, equipment selected, and installation requirements. Government subsidies may reduce the overall investment for eligible residential systems.

How long do solar panels last?

Most premium solar panels come with a performance warranty of up to 25 years and continue generating electricity beyond that with gradual efficiency reduction.

Can solar panels work during cloudy weather?

Yes. Solar panels continue generating electricity even on cloudy days, although output is lower compared to bright sunny conditions.

Is rooftop solar suitable for every home?

Most homes with sufficient roof space and good sunlight exposure are suitable for rooftop solar. A professional site inspection can determine the ideal system size.
