Step-by-Step Process to Apply for Solar Subsidy in Kerala

With electricity costs increasing every year, many homeowners are switching to solar energy to reduce their monthly power bills. The good news is that the Government of India currently offers financial assistance through the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, making rooftop solar more affordable for residential consumers. The scheme provides subsidies of up to ₹78,000 for eligible residential rooftop solar installations.

If you’re planning to install a Solar system for homes in Kottayam, understanding the subsidy application process can help you maximize savings and avoid unnecessary delays.

This guide explains the complete step-by-step process.

Why Apply for a Solar Subsidy?

A rooftop solar system is already a long-term investment that reduces electricity expenses. The subsidy further lowers the initial installation cost, making solar adoption easier for homeowners.

Benefits include:

  • Reduced upfront investment

  • Faster return on investment

  • Lower electricity bills

  • Increased property value

  • Clean and sustainable energy generation

For homeowners considering a Solar system for homes in Kottayam, the subsidy can significantly improve affordability.

Who Is Eligible for the Solar Subsidy?

To qualify for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy scheme, applicants generally must:

  • Be an Indian citizen

  • Own a residential property

  • Have a valid electricity connection

  • Have sufficient rooftop space for solar installation

  • Install the system through an approved vendor listed on the national portal

  • Not have received another rooftop solar subsidy for the same property

Documents Required

Keep the following documents ready before starting the application:

  • Aadhaar Card

  • Latest electricity bill

  • Bank account details

  • Cancelled cheque

  • Mobile number

  • Email address

  • Property ownership proof (if required)

Step 1: Register on the PM Surya Ghar Portal

Visit the official PM Surya Ghar portal and register using:

  • State: Kerala

  • Electricity Distribution Company (KSEB)

  • Consumer Number

  • Mobile Number

  • Email Address

After registration, you can access the rooftop solar application dashboard.

Step 2: Submit Your Rooftop Solar Application

Log in and complete the rooftop solar application form.

You will need to provide:

  • Consumer details

  • Property information

  • Proposed solar system capacity

  • Contact information

Once submitted, the application moves to the feasibility approval stage.

Step 3: Wait for Feasibility Approval

KSEB reviews the application to determine whether your local electricity infrastructure can support the proposed solar installation.

The review includes:

  • Transformer capacity assessment

  • Grid compatibility verification

  • Technical feasibility checks

Approval typically takes a few days depending on the location and application volume.

Step 4: Select an Approved Solar Vendor

After receiving feasibility approval, choose a registered vendor from the approved list available on the portal.

This step is extremely important.

Installing a system through an unapproved vendor may make the project ineligible for subsidy benefits.

If you’re planning a Solar system for homes in Kottayam, working with an experienced installer helps ensure proper design, installation quality, and documentation support.

Step 5: Install the Solar System

Once the vendor is selected, the installation process begins.

This typically includes:

  • Site inspection

  • System design

  • Solar panel installation

  • Inverter installation

  • Electrical connections

  • Safety testing

Professional installation ensures maximum performance and long-term reliability.

Step 6: Apply for Net Metering

After installation, details of the completed system must be uploaded to the portal.

A request for net meter installation is then submitted.

Net metering allows homeowners to:

  • Export excess electricity to the grid

  • Receive energy credits

  • Reduce electricity bills further

This is a crucial step for residential rooftop solar systems.

Step 7: Inspection and Commissioning

KSEB officials inspect the installed solar system to verify:

  • Compliance with technical standards

  • Safety requirements

  • Net meter functionality

After successful verification, a commissioning certificate is issued.

Step 8: Upload Bank Details and Claim Subsidy

Once the commissioning certificate is received:

  • Upload bank account details

  • Upload a cancelled cheque

  • Submit subsidy claim documentation

After verification, the subsidy amount is transferred directly to the applicant’s bank account. Many applicants receive the subsidy within weeks after approval.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Many subsidy delays happen because of avoidable mistakes:

  • Incorrect consumer number

  • Incomplete documentation

  • Selecting unapproved vendors

  • Wrong bank details

  • Delays in uploading installation documents

Ensuring accurate submissions can significantly speed up the process.
