Types of Solar Systems for Homes in Kottayam

1. On-Grid Solar System

This is the most popular option for residential properties.

Benefits include:

  • Lower installation cost

  • Net metering facility

  • No battery maintenance

  • Maximum return on investment

Excess electricity generated during the day is exported to the grid and adjusted against your electricity bill.

2. Hybrid Solar System

Hybrid systems combine solar panels with battery backup.

Suitable for:

  • Areas with frequent power cuts

  • Homes requiring backup power

  • Families seeking energy independence

3. Off-Grid Solar System

These systems operate completely independent of the electricity grid.

Best suited for:

  • Remote locations

  • Farmhouses

  • Areas with limited grid connectivity

Government Subsidy for Residential Solar

Homeowners installing a rooftop solar system for homes in Kottayam can benefit from the PM Surya Ghar subsidy program.

Current subsidy structure includes:

  • 1 kW: Up to ₹30,000 subsidy

  • 2 kW: Up to ₹60,000 subsidy

  • 3 kW and above: Up to ₹78,000 subsidy

The subsidy significantly reduces the overall investment cost and improves ROI.
